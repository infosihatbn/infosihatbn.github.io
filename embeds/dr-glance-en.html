At a glance

✓

Diabetes can damage the tiny blood vessels at the back of your eye.

✓

The early stages usually have no symptoms.

!

A yearly eye screening finds it early, before your sight is affected.

✓

Good control of sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol protects your eyes.

✓

Treatment works best when it starts early.
