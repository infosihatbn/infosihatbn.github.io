At a glance

✓

Glaucoma damages the optic nerve, the cable between your eye and brain.

✓

The most common type gives no warning. Side vision is lost slowly.

✓

Lost vision cannot come back, but treatment can stop or slow the damage.

!

Over 40, or a family member has glaucoma? Get your eyes checked.

✓

A sudden painful red eye with blurred vision is an emergency.
