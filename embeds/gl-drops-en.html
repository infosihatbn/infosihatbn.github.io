How to put in your eye drops

  1. Wash your hands with soap and water.
  2. Tilt your head back and look up.
  3. Gently pull down your lower eyelid to make a small pocket.
  4. Squeeze one drop into the pocket. Do not let the bottle touch your eye.
  5. Close your eye and press gently on the inner corner, near your nose, for 1 to 2 minutes.
  6. Using more than one drop? Wait at least 5 minutes before the next one.

Tip: Use your drops at the same times every day. Bring all your drops to every clinic appointment.
