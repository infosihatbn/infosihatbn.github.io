Inda payah surat rujukan. No referral needed. Datang terus ke mana-mana Klinik Mata Komuniti untuk jumpa doktor mata. Walk in to any Community Eye Clinic to see an eye doctor, a service unique to Brunei.

Kenapa InfoSihat? · Why InfoSihat?

  • Senang difaham · Simple: bahasa yang senang, inda berbelit. Plain words, no medical jargon.
  • Dwibahasa · Bilingual: Bahasa Melayu Brunei dan English.
  • Ditulis doktor · By doctors: ditulis dan disemak oleh doktor di Brunei. Written and reviewed by doctors in Brunei.

Petua · Tip

Ada kencing manis? Periksa mata tiap-tiap tahun, walaupun mata nampak terang.
Have diabetes? Get your eyes checked every year, even if you see well.
