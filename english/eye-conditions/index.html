Eye Conditions

Eye conditions grouped by the part of the eye they affect. Tap a number on the eye, or scroll down to choose a topic. We will add more topics to each group over time.

!

Sudden loss of vision, a very painful red eye, or an eye injury? This is an emergency. Go to the hospital Emergency Department now.

Urgent Conditions

Cataract

The lens sits behind the coloured part of your eye and focuses light. When it turns cloudy, this is called a cataract.

Lens

Cataract

Cloudy, blurred or faded vision that slowly gets worse. Very common with age, and surgery works well.

Glaucoma

The optic nerve carries pictures from your eye to your brain. Glaucoma damages this nerve, often because the pressure inside the eye is too high.

Optic nerve

Glaucoma

The "silent thief of sight" that takes side vision first. Find out who should be checked.

Glaucoma

Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma

Sudden severe eye pain, a red eye, blurred vision, headache and vomiting. Eye pressure rises very fast.

Cornea

The cornea is the clear window at the front of your eye, and the conjunctiva is the thin clear skin over the white of the eye. Both must stay healthy for you to see well.

Cornea

Dry Eye

Gritty, burning or watery eyes? Learn the causes of dry eye and simple treatments that really help.

Cornea

Digital Eye Strain

Tired, dry, sore eyes after long hours on screens. Simple habits like the 20-20-20 rule help.

Cornea

Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

Red, watery or sticky eyes from viruses, bacteria or allergy. Most settle on their own; know the red flags.

Cornea

Pterygium

A fleshy growth from the white of the eye onto the cornea, linked to sun, wind and dust.

Cornea

Contact Lens Safety

Wear and care for lenses safely: no tap water, no sleeping in them, and act fast on a red eye.

Cornea

Corneal Ulcer

An infected sore on the clear window of the eye. Contact lens wearers are most at risk. Needs same-day treatment.

Cornea

Keratoconus

The cornea thins into a cone, blurring vision in young people. Cross-linking can stop it worsening.

Retina

The retina is the thin, light-sensitive layer at the back of the eye, like the film in a camera.

Retina

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetes can damage the back of your eye with no early warning. Yearly screening protects your sight.

Retina

Age-related Macular Degeneration

AMD blurs your central vision after 50. Sudden wavy lines need checking within days.

Retina

Hypertensive Retinopathy

High blood pressure can quietly damage the blood vessels in your eyes. Controlling it protects your sight.

Retina

Retinal Detachment

Flashes, new floaters or a curtain over your vision. The retina is peeling away and needs urgent surgery.

Retina

Endophthalmitis

A serious infection inside the eye, most often days after eye surgery or an eye injection.

Neuro-ophthalmology

Problems with the nerves and brain pathways that control vision, eye movements, the pupils and the eyelids.

Nerves

Ptosis (Droopy Eyelid)

A drooping upper eyelid. Usually due to age, but sometimes a sign of a nerve problem that needs urgent care.

Nerves

Bell's Palsy and Your Eye

Sudden one-sided face weakness can stop the eye closing. Rule out stroke, start treatment early, protect the eye.

Nerves

Eye Nerve Palsies (3rd, 4th, 6th)

Sudden double vision can mean an eye nerve has stopped working. Know when it is an emergency.

Oculoplastics and Orbital Disease

The eyelids, tear ducts and eye socket (orbit) protect the eye and keep it moist.

Eyelids

Blepharitis

Sore, gritty, crusty eyelids? Daily warm compresses and lid cleaning keep this long-term condition under control.

Eyelids

Stye and Chalazion

Painful red or firm painless eyelid lumps. Most settle with warm compresses, so never squeeze them.

Eyelids

Ectropion and Entropion

When the eyelid turns outward (ectropion) or inward (entropion), causing watering, redness and irritation.

Orbit

Thyroid Eye Disease

Bulging, red, gritty eyes or double vision with thyroid problems. Stopping smoking helps; faded vision is urgent.

Uveitis

The uvea is the middle layer of the eye, including the coloured iris. Uveitis is inflammation (swelling) of this layer.

Uvea

Anterior Uveitis

A painful red eye with light sensitivity and blurred vision. Needs prompt treatment to protect your sight.

Paediatric

Children's eyes are still developing. Problems caught early, before about age 7, are much easier to treat.

Children

Myopia in Children

Far things look blurry. Often starts at school age. Time outdoors and myopia control can slow it down.

Children

Amblyopia (Lazy Eye)

Weak vision in one eye that often shows no signs. Early glasses, patching or drops work best before 7–8.

Children

Squint in Children

When the eyes do not point the same way. Early treatment helps prevent a "lazy eye".

Children

Retinoblastoma

A rare eye cancer in young children. A white pupil in flash photos needs an eye check within days.

Eye Injuries

The eye can be hurt by a blow, something sharp, or chemicals. Every eye injury needs to be checked at the Emergency Department.

Eye Injuries

Eye Injuries

Blunt, sharp or chemical injuries to the eye. For chemicals, rinse with clean water for 15 minutes, then go to Emergency.
