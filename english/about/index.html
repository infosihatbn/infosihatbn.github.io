About InfoSihat

InfoSihat: health information at your fingertips.
Maklumat Kesihatan dihujung Jari.

InfoSihat is a free website that explains health conditions in simple, everyday language, in English and Bahasa Melayu Brunei. It is written by doctors in Brunei for the people of Brunei.

Our vision

To empower every individual in Brunei Darussalam with accessible, accurate, and culturally relevant health information, for a healthier and more informed nation.

Our mission

InfoSihat is dedicated to improving health literacy across Brunei by providing culturally appropriate, evidence-based and user-friendly health information. Through digital platforms, community outreach and collaboration with healthcare professionals, we aim to promote informed health choices, disease prevention and community well-being.

Why we started

Health literacy is the ability to find, understand and use health information. In Brunei, many people prefer to read in Malay, yet most reliable health information online is in English, written for other countries, and full of medical terms.

Speaking at the 2nd ASEAN Digital Public Health Conference in 2022, the Minister of Health said:

"Health literacy is still quite low in the country, and it is hoped that digitisation of public health information will help to 'nudge' the public to take better care of their health."

A local study at RIPAS Hospital found that 69.5% of patients with diabetes had inadequate knowledge of their condition. InfoSihat aims to help close that gap, starting with the eye.

What you will find here

  • Clear explanations of eye conditions, starting with diabetic retinopathy and glaucoma
  • Two languages, written to sound like how we actually talk
  • Local context: Brunei research, Brunei services, and Brunei life (including fasting in Ramadan)
  • Pages you can print or show during a clinic visit
  • References at the end of every page

How we write our content

  1. Written by doctors or medical students, in plain language.
  2. Checked by a doctor for accuracy before it is published.
  3. Based on evidence: international guidelines and research, plus Brunei studies where available.
  4. Reviewed regularly: each page shows when it was last reviewed.

Who it is for

Everyone in Brunei, especially Malay-speaking adults, older people, youth and students, caregivers, and families in rural areas.

Important

InfoSihat is for general education only. It does not replace a consultation with your own doctor. If you are worried about your health, please see a doctor. In an emergency, call 991.
