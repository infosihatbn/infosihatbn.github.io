Collaborators

InfoSihat is a team effort by doctors, medical students and volunteers who want health information in Brunei to be clear, trustworthy and easy to read.

Initiative Supervisor

Dr Noor Affizan is a Consultant Ophthalmologist at the Brunei Eye Centre, Ministry of Health. She supervises the InfoSihat initiative and guides its clinical direction. She is President of the Brunei Ophthalmology Society and an Equity Initiative Fellow (2021), a regional programme for leaders working on health equity in Southeast Asia.

During the COVID-19 pandemic she served as Medical Superintendent of the National Isolation Centre, and was named one of the "50 Leading Lights for Kindness & Leadership – Asia Pacific" in 2022. She has led the Ministry of Health's Climate, Health and Sustainability Taskforce, and in 2019 founded You Can Lead BN, an online platform that connects young Bruneians with personal and professional development opportunities.

Initiative Lead

Dr Haziq is an Ophthalmology Resident at the Brunei Eye Centre, Ministry of Health, and the founder of InfoSihat. Before that, Dr Haziq founded the Brunei Medical Students' Association (BMSA) and served as its First President.

Dr Haziq started InfoSihat after seeing how many patients in clinic, especially those more comfortable in Malay, struggled to find health information that was trustworthy, local and easy to understand.

Role in the initiative

  • Vision and direction: sets the overall vision of InfoSihat and decides which health topics come first, starting with eye health.
  • Editor-in-Chief: oversees all content and makes sure every page is accurate, evidence-based and written in plain language.
  • Writing and review: writes and medically reviews the eye health pages, including diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma and the Community Eye Clinics guide.
  • Bilingual and local: makes sure each page works in both Bahasa Melayu Brunei and English, and reflects life and health services in Brunei.
  • Building the team: recruits and guides doctors, medical students and volunteers who write, review, translate and design for InfoSihat.
  • Partnerships and outreach: works with healthcare professionals and the community to share InfoSihat, so patients can use it at home and doctors can use it during consultations.
