Acute Eye Conditions

Some eye problems are emergencies. Treatment within hours can save your sight. Learn the warning signs, so you know when not to wait.

!

Do not wait for a clinic appointment. If you have sudden loss of vision, a curtain or shadow over your vision, a very painful red eye, or worsening pain after eye surgery or an eye injection, go to the hospital Emergency Department now or call 991.

Warning signs to act on today

  • Sudden blurred vision or loss of vision, in one or both eyes
  • A shower of new floaters, flashes of light, or a dark curtain or shadow
  • A very painful red eye, especially with headache, vomiting or rainbow haloes around lights
  • Pain, redness and worse vision in the days after eye surgery or an eye injection
  • Any injury to the eye, or chemicals in the eye (rinse with plenty of clean water first)
  • Sudden double vision or a suddenly drooping eyelid

Acute conditions

Retina

Retinal Detachment

Flashes, new floaters or a curtain over your vision. The retina is peeling away and needs urgent surgery.

Eye pressure

Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma

Sudden severe eye pain, a red eye, blurred vision, headache and vomiting. Eye pressure rises very fast.

Infection

Endophthalmitis

A serious infection inside the eye, most often days after eye surgery or an eye injection.

Not an emergency, but worried?

You can walk in to any Community Eye Clinic during office hours to see an eye doctor, no referral letter needed.
