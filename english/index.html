Tip of the day

  • 🩸

    Have diabetes? Get your eyes checked every year, even if you see well.

  • 📱

    Using a screen for a long time? Every 20 minutes, look at something 6 metres away for 20 seconds.

  • 🕶️

    Out in the sun? Wear sunglasses that block UV light, and a hat.

  • 🌳

    Let children play outdoors every day. It helps protect them from short-sightedness.

  • 🧼

    Wash your hands before touching your eyes or putting in eye drops.

  • 🚭

    Stopping smoking lowers your risk of cataract and other eye diseases.

  • 👪

    Glaucoma in the family? Have your eyes checked regularly from age 40.

  • 🥽

    Cutting grass, drilling or hammering? Wear safety goggles.

  • 🚨

    Sudden loss of vision, flashes, or a dark curtain in your vision? Go to the Emergency Department the same day.

  • 💊

    Only use steroid eye drops if a doctor prescribed them. Long use can cause glaucoma and cataract.

  • 👀

    Child squinting, or sitting very close to the TV? Get their eyes checked.

  • 🌿

    Never put breast milk, herbs or traditional remedies in the eye. They can cause serious infection.

  • 😴

    Take out contact lenses before sleeping, and never rinse them with tap water.

  • 🥗

    Eat plenty of leafy greens, fruit and fish. Good for your whole body, eyes included.

  • 🩺

    Check your blood pressure regularly. High blood pressure can harm your eyes, heart and kidneys.

  • 🏃

    Aim for 30 minutes of activity on most days, such as brisk walking.

  • 🥤

    Choose plain water over sugary drinks. It helps keep your blood sugar healthy.

  • 🌙

    Fasting in Ramadan? Do not stop your glaucoma drops. Ask your doctor about the best times to use them.

  • 🏥

    Community Eye Clinics in Brunei are walk-in. No referral needed.

  • 🚗

    Glare from car headlights at night getting worse? It could be a cataract. Get your eyes checked.

  • 🧪

    Chemical splash in the eye? Rinse with clean water for at least 15 minutes, then go to the Emergency Department.
